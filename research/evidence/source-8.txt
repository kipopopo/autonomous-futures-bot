### What do "maker" and "taker" mean?

#### **Taker:**

A “taker” trade happens when you place an order that executes immediately, partially or fully against existing orders on the Global Orderbook.

Market Orders are always taker trades because they remove liquidity from the market. Takers “take” volume off the Global Orderbook.

#### **Maker:**

A “maker” trade occurs when you place an order that adds liquidity to the Global Orderbook by resting there until it is matched with another order. Limit Orders that do not execute immediately are examples of maker orders. Makers ”make the market” by providing volume that others can trade against.

### How to calculate the trading fee for COIN-Margined contracts?

Trading Fee = Position Value * trading fee

Position Value = (Contract Size*Contract Multiplier) / execution price

For example, a Regular User's maker fee is 0.02% and a Regular User’s taker fee is 0.05%

The Regular User buys 10 BTCUSD 0925 COIN-M Delivery Futures by making a Market Order:

Position Value = (Contract Size*Contract Multiplier) / execution price

= (10 “contracts” * 100 USD) / 10,104 USD

= 0.09897 BTC

Taker fee: 0.09897 * 0.050% = 0.000049485 BTC

After the price increases, the Regular User sells 10 BTCUSD 0925 COIN-M Delivery Futures by making a Limit Order:

Position Value = (Contract Size*Contract Multiplier) / execution price

= (10 “contracts” * 100 USD) / 11,104 USD

= 0.09 BTC

Maker fee: 0.09 * 0.02% = 0.000018 BTC

### How to calculate trading fee of USDS-Margined contracts?

Trading Fee = Position Value * trading fee

Position Value = Contract Size * execution price

For example, a Regular User’s maker fee is 0.02% and a Regular User’s taker fee is 0.05%

The Regular User buys 1 BTC worth of BTCUSDT USDS-M Perpetual Futures Contract by making a Market Order:

Position Value = Contract Size * execution price

= 1 BTC * 10,104

= 10,104 USDT

Taker fee is paid: 10,104 * 0.05% = 5.052 USDT

After the price rises, the Regular User sells 1 BTC worth of BTCUSDT USDS-M Perpetual Futures C using a Limit Order:

Position Value = Contract Size * execution price

= 1 BTC * 11,104

= 11,104 USDT

Maker fee is paid: 11,104 * 0.02% = 2.2208 USDT
