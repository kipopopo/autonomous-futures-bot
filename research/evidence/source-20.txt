* Investor Alert List

INVESTOR ALERT LIST

This list contains the names of unauthorised entities and individuals. You are advised not to deal with or invest through them. If you invest through unauthorised entities and individuals, you will not be protected under Malaysian securities laws.  
  

Some entities in this list may impersonate legitimate entities which are described as “potential clone”.   
  
This list is not exhaustive and is based on the information available to the SC at the time of publication.   
  
If you have any doubt, please call us at [0362048999](tel:+60362048999) or email us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#dcbdb8a9bdb29cafb9bfbfb3b1f2bfb3b1f2b1a5)
